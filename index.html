About

Two decades on the wall.

Sheela is a muralist and graffiti artist based in Atlanta, GA, with two decades of experience producing large-scale public murals and invitational graffiti lettering across the U.S. and internationally. Her work spans city-commissioned murals, festival walls, and mentorship programs for emerging and teenage artists — always built from the same starting point: spray paint, a wall, and a deadline.

She has been a sponsored artist for Loop Colors spray paint since 2021, and her work has been featured in the graffiti book "Stay Gold" and China's "Book of Style," alongside interviews for the Museum of Graffiti, Bombing Science, and Killa Kela Podcast, among others.
